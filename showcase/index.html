The system

One visual language.
Everywhere.

ItundaFace takes the system-level ideas that make a large emoji library coherent—simple geometry, optical balance, shared direction, angle, palette and viewpoint—and gives them an original Itunda identity.

01

Simple forms

Circles, lines and controlled curves. Detail only earns its place when it survives at product scale.

02

One visual scale

Every glyph is optically balanced so a heart, face, object or place can sit together without feeling mismatched.

03

One world

Flat and 3D share the same silhouette, while a compact palette and consistent viewpoint make the family feel related.

3D experience

Touch the expression.

Drag the ItundaFace around. Switch expressions. Use the controls to snap the camera and rotation. This is a lightweight browser experience built from the actual 3D assets.
