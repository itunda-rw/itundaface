# ItundaFace Finance

Finance glyphs describe money movement and payment actions without reproducing third-party provider marks.

## Rwanda-native vocabulary

- `mobile-money.svg` — mobile money account/payment concept
- `wallet.svg` — stored-value or wallet concept
- `bill-payment.svg` — bill or utility payment
- `airtime.svg` — airtime top-up
- `merchant-payment.svg` — person-to-merchant payment
- `transport-payment.svg` — transport fare/payment
- `interoperable-payment.svg` — payment moving between participating rails
- `government-payment.svg` — government-service payment

## Payment interactions

- `send.svg` — money sent to another person or account
- `receive.svg` — money received
- `cash-in.svg` — value added to an account or wallet
- `cash-out.svg` — value withdrawn from an account or wallet
- `qr-payment.svg` — QR-based payment action
- `refund.svg` — money returned after a transaction
- `receipt.svg` — payment receipt or proof of transaction
- `transaction-history.svg` — transaction timeline/history

Matching 3D variants live under `../3d/finance/`.

The vocabulary reflects Rwanda's digital-payment environment, including mobile money, merchant payments, government-service payments, and interoperable payment infrastructure such as eKash. ItundaFace does not use MTN, Airtel, eKash, bank, or government logos in these glyphs.

Use flat variants for compact UI and 3D variants for prominent moments. Preserve the semantic meaning and use Itunda indigo selectively as the identity accent.
