# Changelog

## 2.1.0 — 2026-09-16

### Added
- Rwanda-native culture glyphs: Agaseke, Umuganda, and Umuganura, with flat and 3D variants.
- Product-state family covering success, verified, pending, warning, error, locked, processing, delivered, and completed states.
- Rwanda-native finance vocabulary for mobile money, wallet, bill payment, airtime, merchant payment, transport payment, interoperable payment, and government-service payment.
- Flat and 3D finance variants with shared silhouettes and material grammar.

### Finance design
- Semantic finance colors remain meaning-first; Itunda indigo is used selectively as the identity accent.
- Provider-specific marks and logos are intentionally excluded from generic finance glyphs.
- Interoperable-payment vocabulary reflects Rwanda's national interoperable-payment direction without reproducing eKash artwork.

## 2.0.0 — 2026-09-16

### Added
- Master reaction family with flat and 3D variants.
- Communication glyph family.
- Places, maps, and mobility glyph family.
- Identity and security glyph family.
- Commerce and marketplace glyph family.
- Payments and finance glyph family.
- Rwanda-native culture and celebration glyph family.
- Shared 2D/3D construction, optical-size, material, and motion rules.
- Accessibility labels and reduced-motion guidance.

### Design system
- Canonical Itunda indigo identity accent: `#7472F4` light / `#7675F8` dark.
- Flat glyphs target 14–24px inline sizes.
- 3D glyphs target 32–64px+ key-visual sizes.
- Flat and 3D variants preserve the same underlying silhouette.
- Directional objects default to right-facing / left-to-right flow.
- Deliberate object tilts default to 45° clockwise.

### Originality
ItundaFace is independently authored. TossFace is used only as a reference for visual-system discipline; no TossFace artwork, vectors, or modified assets are included.
